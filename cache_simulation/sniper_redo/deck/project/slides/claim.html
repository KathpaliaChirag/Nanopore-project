What we can and cannot claim

We can say

  • In this simulator, LLC and L2 associativity, replacement policy and size did not speed kraken2 up
  • A hardware prefetcher did, on every setup we ran: 3% to 25%
  • The gain was larger with more cores

We cannot say

  • That the same holds for the full 1.87 M-read job: our runs are tiny and the LLC is never reused
  • That Luna would gain this much: the baseline has no prefetcher and Luna's CPU already has its own
  • Why the gain grows with cores

Next

  • Run kraken2 on Luna with the CPU's prefetchers off and on
  • Repeat 8 and 16 cores with a few hundred reads each
Direction is the finding; sizes are indicative. Sniper is not calibrated against Luna's performance counters. All data and the figure script are in cache_simulation/sniper_redo in the repository.
