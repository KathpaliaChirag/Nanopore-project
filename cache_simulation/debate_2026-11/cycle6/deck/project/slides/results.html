The cache wins on small workloads and loses once the job passes about 2 to 5 million bases. Total bases drove the flip; read length did not.

Left: full grid in the study notes (4-way is the best or tied-best width in every row at 50 reads). Right: the long-read scaling run, nested prefixes of one file at roughly constant read length. At 2,000 reads on the 50 MB database every cache variant was slower than none (4-way 1.28x slower, 16-way 1.39x slower). All values are simulated cycles; see slide 3 for why they are weak evidence about the real machine.
